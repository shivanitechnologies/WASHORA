Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What happens if it rains during or right after my wash?

We provide a 24-hour Clean Car Rain Guarantee! If sudden Chennai showers soil your freshly washed vehicle within 24 hours of completion, simply let us know via the app and we will dispatch a free exterior touch-up!

Do you need access to our apartment water tap or power socket?

Never. Our custom mobile vans carry an on-board 200-litre reverse osmosis deionized water tank and a low-decibel generator. We do not disturb your residential utility lines or neighbors.

What payment methods are supported?

We support online payment at booking (UPI, Google Pay, Credit/Debit Cards, Net Banking) or Doorstep Pay upon job completion by scanning the technician’s QR code or paying cash.
