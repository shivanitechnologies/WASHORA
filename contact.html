Support & Booking FAQs

Quick clarity on doorstep logistics and technician arrival.

How do I give gate permission to the technician at my apartment?

When your booking is confirmed, you will receive your technician's name, phone number, and van registration plate number (e.g. TN 09 AZ 4521) in the app and via SMS. You can easily pre-approve entry on MyGate, NoBrokerHood, or ApnaComplex.

What happens if it rains during or right after my wash?

We provide a 24-hour Clean Car Rain Guarantee! If sudden Chennai showers soil your freshly washed vehicle within 24 hours of completion, simply let us know via the app and we will dispatch a free exterior touch-up!

Do you need access to our apartment water tap or power socket?

Never. Our custom mobile vans carry an on-board 200-litre reverse osmosis deionized water tank and a low-decibel generator. We do not disturb your residential utility lines or neighbors.

Can I pay with Cash or UPI after inspecting my vehicle?

Yes, absolutely. We support "Doorstep Pay." You only scan the technician’s QR code or hand over payment once you have inspected the paint shine and clean interior.
