Pro Car Cleaning Tips

Small habits that keep your car immaculate between professional doorstep detailing visits.

Use Two Buckets

Always rinse your mitt in a fresh water bucket before re-dipping into soapy water. This traps sand at the bottom and prevents scratch swirls.

Microfiber Towel Rules

Never wash microfiber towels with fabric softener, as it clogs the microscopic split fibers and destroys their water-absorption capacity.

Avoid Dish Soaps

Household dishwashing liquid contains aggressive degreasers that strip carnauba wax and dry out clear coat resins. Use pH-neutral automotive shampoo.

Never Wash in Direct Sun

Sunlight evaporates water drops rapidly, leaving concentrated mineral and calcium deposits that bake into clear coat etch marks.

Climate Protection

Seasonal Car Care in Chennai

How to protect your vehicle against extreme coastal heat, torrential monsoon humidity, and winter morning fog.

Summer Heat & UV Defense

Chennai summer temperatures can push dashboard surface heat past 65°C. Apply weekly UV vinyl conditioner to avoid dashboard cracking and ceramic wax to prevent clear coat chalking.

  • • Use reflective windshield sunshades
  • • Apply leather moisturizing conditioner
  • • Check coolant and wiper fluid levels

Monsoon Anti-Rust & Mud Care

Puddle water carries industrial runoff and salt. Regular high-pressure underbody washes prevent rust corrosion, while hydrophobic glass coatings maintain 100% windshield vision during heavy downpours.

  • • Apply rain-repellent windshield coating
  • • Bi-weekly undercarriage mud flushing
  • • Steam clean AC cabin filters to kill damp mold

Winter Morning Dew & Clarity

Morning condensation collects air pollution particles that stick to paint. Wipe down morning dew with plush microfiber mitts and restore yellowed headlight lenses for crisp night and fog visibility.

  • • Headlight UV de-oxidation treatment
  • • Interior anti-fog glass treatment
  • • Tyre pressure calibration for cold tarmac
