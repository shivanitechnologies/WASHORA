Transparent Policy

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our billing, refunds, and guarantees.

Can I cancel or pause my subscription anytime?

Yes, absolutely. There are no lock-in contracts or cancellation fees. You can pause your plan whenever you travel or cancel instantly from your Customer Dashboard. Any remaining washes will stay active in your wallet for 60 days.

What happens if I miss a scheduled weekly wash?

Your wash is never lost! We have an automatic rollover policy. If you reschedule with at least 2 hours' notice or skip a week, that wash credits automatically to your next billing cycle.

Do prices include GST and technician gratuity?

All prices listed include 18% GST with GST-compliant tax invoices provided immediately in your customer portal. Tipping your technician is entirely optional.

Is there an additional charge for large SUVs or 7-seaters?

Standard pricing covers hatchbacks and standard sedans. Full-size 7-seater SUVs (e.g., Toyota Fortuner, MG Gloster) incur a nominal ₹150 size surcharge to cover the additional snow foam volume and detailing labor.
