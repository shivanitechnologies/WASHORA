Terms & Conditions

Please read these terms carefully before booking our doorstep car care treatments.

1. Service Scope & Doorstep Access

CAR SHINE delivers professional mobile detailing directly to residential apartments, independent homes, and office parking lots across approved Chennai postal codes. The customer must ensure that the mobile detailing van has safe entry and parking clearance within approximately 15 to 20 meters of the booked vehicle.

2. 100% Self-Contained Rig Guarantee

Our customized mobile vans are engineered with independent 250-liter demineralized soft water tanks, Honda ultra-quiet inverted power generators, and high-pressure steam washers. We do NOT require customer electrical plug points or water hose connections, leaving zero municipal dependency.

3. Customer Personal Belongings

While our certified technicians undergo rigorous police verification and background checks, customers are strongly required to remove all valuable items, cash, credit cards, high-end electronics, and personal documents from the vehicle cabin prior to interior deep cleaning. CAR SHINE assumes no liability for unaccounted loose items left inside gloveboxes or seat pockets.

4. 24-Hour Rain Shine Guarantee

All Full Car Detailing and Ceramic Coating packages include our signature 24-Hour Rain Shine Guarantee. If unexpected rain or monsoon showers soil your vehicle within 24 hours of service completion, contact our dispatch hotline for a complimentary exterior waterless spot wipe down.

5. Governing Law & Jurisdiction

These terms and conditions are governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Republic of India. Any legal disputes arising out of doorstep detailing operations shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the civil courts in Chennai, Tamil Nadu.
