Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 1, 2026 • CAR SHINE Technologies Private Limited, Chennai, India.

1. Introduction & Commitment

At CAR SHINE ("we", "our", or "us"), we value and respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, store, utilize, and protect your personal and automotive data when you access our doorstep car wash website, mobile booking web app, and related services across Chennai and surrounding territories.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Name, phone number, email address, and optional password credentials.
  • Vehicle Information: Vehicle manufacturer, model, exterior color, registration plate number, and body type (Hatchback, Sedan, SUV, Luxury).
  • Doorstep Service Address: Apartment/flat number, street name, neighborhood, PIN code, and GPS coordinates provided for doorstep dispatch.
  • Payment Records: Transaction reference IDs, invoice amounts, payment method (UPI, Card, Doorstep Cash). We do NOT store complete credit/debit card numbers.

3. Vehicle Photography & Quality Assurance

Our certified detailing technicians capture pre-wash and post-wash digital photographs of your vehicle's exterior panels. This visual documentation serves exclusively for:

  • Documenting pre-existing scratches, swirl marks, or paint oxidation before detailing begins.
  • Verifying 100% spotless execution and customer quality satisfaction report.
  • Photographs of vehicle interior personal belongings are strictly prohibited under technician protocol.

4. Data Storage & Security Protocols

Your data is transmitted using modern SSL/TLS encryption. Booking states and customer profiles are maintained securely without unauthorized third-party disclosure. We never sell, rent, or trade your personal or vehicle data to marketing agencies or advertisers.

5. Customer Rights & Account Erasure

Under applicable Indian Digital Personal Data Protection laws, you retain the full right to review, update, or permanently delete your stored vehicles, bookings history, and personal contact details by reaching out to our data grievance desk at support@carshine.com.
