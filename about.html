Our Story

Born from a Frustration with Dusty Queues and Dirty Wash Cloths.

In 2022, we watched apartment parking lots where local daily cleaners wiped luxury and family cars with single dirty buckets and sandy rags. That friction causes deep paint swirl marks and permanent paint dullness.

We decided to change everything. We custom-engineered mobile detailing vans equipped with 250-liter soft demineralized water tanks, whisper-quiet Honda generators, German pressure washers, and safe pH-neutral snow foams. Today, CAR SHINE brings car-spa precision directly to your home.

Service Execution

Our Doorstep Work Process

Three stages that guarantee a swirl-free, showroom finish every time.

01

Pre-Wash Snow Foam Soak

We cover your car in thick snow foam to soften dirt and road grime, lifting sand grains away without physical contact to eliminate paint swirls.

02

Interior Steam & Scrub

We vacuum every crevice, steam-clean AC vents to eliminate bacteria, condition leather seats, and apply UV protection to the dashboard.

03

Shine Seal & Handover

We apply hydrophobic sealant to protect your clear coat, gloss the tyres, and invite you to inspect the vehicle before digital sign-off.
