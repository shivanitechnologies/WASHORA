Our Story

Born from a Frustration with Dirty Wash Buckets.

In 2022, our founders noticed a recurring tragedy across apartment complexes in Chennai: local cleaners wiping expensive cars with dirty, grit-filled rags, grinding coarse dust right into the paint and leaving permanent spiderweb swirls.

CAR SHINE was created to provide a studio-grade alternative. We engineered specialized mobile detailing vans equipped with pure reverse-osmosis water systems, silent Honda generators, Italian snow foam cannons, and certified technicians who treat every car like their own.
